Behind the Scientist
A story of biological curiosity meeting computational power.

I'm Debojyoti Das Thakur, an MSc Bioinformatics student at Garden City University, driven by an unyielding passion for understanding life at its most fundamental — molecular — level.

My journey began in the microbiology laboratories of Siliguri College, where I built hands-on expertise in biological experimentation, staining techniques, microbial culture, and molecular assays. That foundation of wet-lab precision now fuels my pursuit of computational biology.

Today I stand at the intersection of genomics, AI, and healthcare — exploring how machine learning can decode the complexity of multi-omics data, predict cancer pathways, and transform medicine into a precision science.
